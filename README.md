# Jey Puget Gil
 
**Postdoctoral researcher · Serious games, AI & knowledge graphs · Grenoble**
 
I am a person passionate about science, society and art!
 
- 🔬 From December 2026, I am a postdoctoral researcher at [LIG](https://www.liglab.fr) (Université Grenoble Alpes), in the [SocSim-K](https://lig-socsim-k.imag.fr) team, within the [TrAI-Soc chair](https://cerag.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/en/chairs/chair-training-soc-miai-cluster-ia) of the MIAI Cluster: I design and evaluate a serious game that helps students in precarious situations regain agency over their food.
- 🎓 I hold a Ph.D. in computer science from [LIRIS](https://liris.cnrs.fr/en/thesis/thesis-jey-puget-gil) ([BD](https://liris.cnrs.fr/en/team/bd) and [Origami](https://liris.cnrs.fr/en/team/origami) teams), defended in June 2026: [*Querying of Multi-Version RDF Knowledge Graphs through Condensed Representations*](https://theses.hal.science/tel-05695880).
- 🧑‍🏫 I was a research and teaching associate (ATER) at Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 (2025–2026).
- 👯 I maintain and co-develop the platform of our association "[Comförting, your bubble](https://comforting.xyz)" in React.js!
- 🌐 My personal website: [hemoreg.me](https://hemoreg.me/en)
